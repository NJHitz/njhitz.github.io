18U Staff
Amanda DiZinno
18U Assistant Coach
Moravian University (DIII)
Mount Olive HS Alum
Corner Infield / Defense

Amanda is a 2009 graduate of Mount Olive High School, where she played varsity softball from 2006–2009, winning both a conference championship and a state sectional title. She earned multiple All-Conference and All-County honors and joined the 100 Hits Club with 129 career hits.

She went on to play at Moravian University from 2010–2013, appearing in 162 career games. Moravian captured three conference championships, a regional championship, and made a 2010 Division III College World Series appearance, with that team later inducted into the Moravian Hall of Fame. Amanda recorded 141 career hits and held single-season and career put-out records for both Moravian and the Landmark Conference. She has coached with the NJ Hitz 18U program since 2020.

  • Mount Olive varsity standout & 100 Hits Club (129 hits)
  • Conference & state sectional champion at Mount Olive HS
  • Moravian University DIII athlete (162 career games)
  • 2010 DIII College World Series appearance & Hall of Fame team member
  • 18U NJ Hitz coach since 2020
